# LD

## What is LD?

LD is a private workspace for planning and organizing music performances. It brings songs, artists, groups, line distributions, and dance formations into one place, making it easier to shape a performance from its first lyric to its final formation.

### What you can do

- Create songs from YouTube videos and structure their lyrics into sections, lines, and parts.
- Organize artists into groups and keep track of their roles, colors, and performance details.
- Build and refine line distributions for a selected song and group.
- Plan dance formations alongside each distribution.
- Use **The Search** to manage contestants, explore card libraries, and simulate a survival show.
